## 4. WEAKEST FIVE ASPECTS & IMPROVEMENT NOTES

1. **External Buyer Checkout Flow Not Recorded Live (`02:00–02:14`):**
   While the PayPal invoice dispatch and recipient URL (`sandbox.paypal.com/invoice/p/#...`) are verified, the physical login of a buyer account completing checkout on PayPal's hosted page is represented via sandbox status proof rather than a live buyer screencast. Disclosed honestly, but remains a limitation.
2. **Fast Pacing in Invoice Creation (`01:00–01:22`):**
   The invoice creation scene covers both line item authoring and saved details in 22 seconds. Judges who want to examine every tax calculation field must pause the video.
3. **PDF Page Display is Static (`02:14–02:19`):**
   The invoice PDF is displayed cleanly as an itemized sheet, but an animated cursor clicking "Download PDF" would make the interaction feel even more tactile.
4. **Reminder Draft Feature is Summarized (`02:19–02:25`):**
   The client-aware reminder modal (`src/client/components/ReminderModal.tsx`) with its polite/firm/final tone settings is mentioned in the activity log rather than given a dedicated full-screen walkthrough due to the 176-second runtime constraint.
5. **No Live Spoken Human Demo Voiceover:**
   The narration uses Microsoft Neural Christopher at +6%. While crystal clear and broadcast-balanced (-18.5 dB), a live human speaker video intro in picture-in-picture could add personal charisma for non-technical judges.
